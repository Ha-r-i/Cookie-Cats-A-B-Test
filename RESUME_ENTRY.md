# Resume project entry

**Cookie Cats Player Retention A/B Test** | Python, pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib

Public-data reproduction | November 2026

[GitHub](https://github.com/Ha-r-i/Cookie-Cats-A-B-Test)

- Reproduced a public A/B test on 90,189 players, validating assignment data and comparing Day 1 and Day 7 retention.
- Applied two-proportion tests, 95% confidence intervals, and 10,000 bootstrap resamples; measured a 0.82 percentage point decrease in Day 7 retention at gate 40 (p = 0.0016).
- Recommended retaining gate 30, supported by sensitivity checks and an allocation diagnostic; documented missing revenue data and assignment-log limitations.
